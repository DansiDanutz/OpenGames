# For assistants

- Making a game: follow `.claude/skills/make-game/SKILL.md`.
- Sending it: follow `.claude/skills/publish-game/SKILL.md`.
- Support, sponsors and posts: follow `.claude/skills/grow-game/SKILL.md`.
- A creator changes only `games/<their game>` and its test. `library.lock.json`, `kit/`, `app/` and `server/` belong to the maintainers.
- Never approve a game (`tools/approve.js`) for its own creator.
