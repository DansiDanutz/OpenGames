OpenGames

No more middleman.

You and the other player connect to each other. A small server only introduces you, and carries your moves when a direct connection is not possible.

Games

    A direct connection shows your address to the person you play. With this on, your moves go through the server instead.

    A ranked match shows your name on the leaderboard and sends its signed moves to the match server, which checks them. The other player sees this before the match.
